# Frost-Moth Lantern Catcher

Help Pip the mouse guide glowing moths into his warm winter lantern while dodging heavy frost crystals. A cozy, glowing arcade experience inspired by quiet winter nights in Loki's Town.

**How to play:** Move Pip with your Mouse, Touch, or Arrow Keys to catch moths and avoid frost.

▶️ Play it: https://lokistown.github.io/games/frost-moth-lantern-catcher/
